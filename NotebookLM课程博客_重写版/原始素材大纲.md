- （cell 11）`def calculate_split_frequency_bin(split_frequency, sample_rate, num_frequency_bins):` `"""Infer the frequency bin associated to a given split frequency."""` `frequency_range = sample_rate / 2` `frequency_delta_per_bin = frequency_range / num_frequency_bins` `split_frequency_bin = math.floor(split_frequency / frequency_delta_per_bin)` `return int(split_frequency_bin)`
